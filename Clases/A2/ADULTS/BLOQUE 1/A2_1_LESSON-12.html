📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Story Toolkit 📚
START
Here are some important moments in my life. My life story is simple.
CONNECT
First, I ___. Then, I ___. Later, I ___. Finally, I ___.
ADD TIME
in 2015 · when I was 18 · a few years ago · last year
PAST EVENTS
finished school · started a course/first job · moved to ___ · started learning English · learned a new skill · visited ___ · achieved a goal
ADD DETAIL
I was nervous/excited. It was important to me. I learned a lot.
CLOSE
These moments were important to me. I learned a lot from these experiences.
PARTNER
Really? Why was that important? How did you feel? What did you learn?
