👀 7 · Visual Focus
Arrangement or Now? 🔤
🕐 NOW
I'm working now.
I'm having coffee now.
📅 TOMORROW
I'm working tomorrow morning.
I'm having coffee with Camila tomorrow at 1:00.
KEY IDEA
Future time + organized activity = future arrangement
REMEMBER: Tomorrow + organized activity = future arrangement.

📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Tomorrow Toolkit 📚
ASK
What are you doing tomorrow? Are you working tomorrow?
ANSWER
I'm working tomorrow morning. I'm meeting a friend in the afternoon. I'm having dinner at 7:00.
TIME
tomorrow, tomorrow morning/afternoon/evening, tonight, after work, after class, at 6:00
DETAILS
with my family, with a friend, at work, at a café, at home, online
KEEP TALKING
What time? Where? Who with? Really? Sounds good! Sounds busy!

🗣️ 14 · Free Production Support
Keep the conversation going! 🗣️
START / ARRANGEMENT
What are you doing tomorrow? I'm ___ tomorrow morning/evening.
ACTIVITIES
working, studying, meeting someone, having lunch/dinner, visiting family, exercising, staying home
ADD TIME / DETAIL
at ___, after work, in the morning. with ___, online
ASK MORE
What time? Where? Who with? What about the evening?
IF STUCK
1. Choose an activity. 2. Add a time. 3. Keep speaking.
YOU CAN USE THE SCREEN. COMMUNICATION > MEMORIZATION
