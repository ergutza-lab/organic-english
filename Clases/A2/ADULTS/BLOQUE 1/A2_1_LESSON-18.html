Symptom Toolkit 📚
GENERAL
I don't feel well. I feel sick. I feel tired. I feel dizzy.
I HAVE...
a headache · a stomachache · a cough · a sore throat · a fever · a cold
___ HURTS
My head hurts. My throat hurts. My stomach hurts. My back hurts.
ASK
Are you okay? What's wrong? Do you have a ___? Does your ___ hurt? Anything else?
DETAILS
a little · pretty bad · today · this morning · since yesterday
EMPATHY
I'm sorry. Oh no. I hope you feel better soon.
DESCRIBE — DON'T DIAGNOSE
