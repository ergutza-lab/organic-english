👀 7 · Visual Focus
Build a good memory 🧩
1. WHEN?
Last month... Last weekend... On Saturday...
2. PERSON / PLACE
I visited ___. I went with ___. I went to ___.
3. WHAT DID YOU DO?
I ___. We ___. Choose 3 past actions.
4. ADD DETAILS
with my family · with a friend · at home · there
5. CLOSE
We had a great time. I really liked that day.
YOU DO NOT NEED A LONG STORY.
⏱️ 4 min

Useful Phrases 📚

PERSON / PLACE
I visited ___. I went to ___. I went with ___.
PAST ACTIONS
visited · talked · walked · watched · cooked · listened · went · had · ate · saw · bought · took
CLOSE
We had a great time. I really liked that day. I really enjoyed it.
KEEP TALKING
Really? Nice! What else did you do? Why is it a good memory? What about you?
