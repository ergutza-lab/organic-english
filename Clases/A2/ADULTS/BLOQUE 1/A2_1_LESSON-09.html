👀 7 · Visual Focus
WAS or WERE? 🔤
WAS — I · He · She · It
I was quiet.
My school was small.
My favorite activity was drawing.
WERE — You · We · They
We were happy.
My friends were funny.
My teachers were friendly.
ASK
What were you like? · What was your school like? · Were you shy?
REMEMBER: Use WAS / WERE to describe the past.
⏱️ 4 min
📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Useful Phrases 📚
START
When I was a child... When I was younger...
ME
I was quiet. I was active. I was curious. I was friendly.
PLACE
My school was ___. My neighborhood was ___. My home was ___.
PEOPLE
My teacher was ___. My teachers were ___. My friends were ___. We were ___.
FAVORITES
My favorite activity/game/subject was ___.
ASK
What were you like? Were you shy? What was your school like? What were your friends like?
KEEP TALKING
Really? Me too. That's interesting. What about you?
