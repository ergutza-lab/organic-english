📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Problem & Help Toolkit 📚
START
Excuse me. I have a problem. There's a problem with ___.
DOESN'T WORK
My key card doesn't work. The ticket machine doesn't work.
ISN'T WORKING
The Wi-Fi isn't working.
I CAN'T
I can't open my room. I can't find my gate. I can't find my bag. I can't connect to the Wi-Fi.
ASK FOR HELP
Can you help me, please? Could you help me, please?
HELPER — ASK MORE
What's the problem? What room are you in? Which gate do you need? What's your name?
HELPER — RESPOND
Of course. Let me check. I can help you. Here's a new key card. Go to Desk 3. Please wait here.
REACT
Okay. Great. Perfect. Thank you.

🗣️ 14 · Free Production Support
Keep the Conversation Going! 🗣️
START
Excuse me. I have a problem.
SAY WHAT'S WRONG
My ___ doesn't work. The ___ isn't working. I can't find ___. I can't open ___. I can't connect to ___.
ADD ONE DETAIL
It's room ___. I need Gate ___. I tried again, but it still doesn't work. I looked near ___.
ASK FOR HELP
Can you help me, please? Could you help me, please?
HELPER — ASK MORE
What's the problem? What room are you in? Which gate do you need? Where did you look?
HELPER — RESPOND
Of course. Let me check. I can help you. Try this. Here's a new ___. Go to ___. Please wait here.
RESPOND
Okay. Great. Perfect. Thank you.
IF YOU GET STUCK
1. Say I have a problem. 2. Say WHAT is wrong. 3. Add ONE detail. 4. Say Can you help me, please? 5. Answer one question. 6. Say Thank you.
