Useful Phrases 📚
CHOOSE YOUR ROUTE
FIRST JOB
My first job was at ___. I worked at ___. My manager was ___. My coworkers were ___. I helped/prepared/cleaned ___.
SCHOOL / STUDY
My first school was ___. My teacher was ___. My classmates were ___. I studied/practiced/talked to ___.
FEELING / DESCRIBE
I was nervous/excited/happy/shy. It was small/busy/interesting/difficult.
REFLECT
I learned ___. I liked the experience. I remember it because ___.
ASK
What was it like? What did you do? Were you nervous? What did you learn?
