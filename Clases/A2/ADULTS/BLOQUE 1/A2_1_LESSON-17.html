Talk about your habits! 🗣️
Speak for about 45–60 seconds each.
INCLUDE
2 or 3 everyday habits
+
At least 2 frequency expressions
+
One habit you feel good about · One habit you may want to improve

NO MEDICAL ADVICE · COMMUNICATION > PERFECT GRAMMAR · Real or safe fictional information is okay.
