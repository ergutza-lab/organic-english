📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Useful Phrases 📚
ASK PLACE
Where did you go? When? Who did you go with?
ANSWER
I went to ___. I went with ___.
ASK EXPERIENCE
What did you do there? What did you eat/see/buy? Did you take photos?
PAST ACTIONS
went · had · ate · saw · bought · took
KEEP TALKING
Really? Nice! What else? What about you?
REMEMBER: Where did you GO? I WENT to ___.
