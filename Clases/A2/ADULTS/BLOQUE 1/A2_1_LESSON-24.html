🔍 6 · Look at the conversation!
Comparative or Superlative? 🧩
TWO PLACES → COMPARATIVE
Riverside Park is quieter than Central Plaza.
quiet → quieter than
THREE PLACES → SUPERLATIVE
Riverside Park · Central Plaza · Old Town
Riverside Park is the quietest place.
quiet → the quietest
LONGER ADJECTIVE
Riverside Park is more relaxing than Central Plaza. ↓ Riverside Park is the most relaxing place.
SPECIAL
good → better → the best

👀 7 · Visual Focus
Recommendation Toolkit 📚
RECOMMEND
I recommend ___. You should visit ___. I think ___ is a great place to visit. ___ is a good choice.
COMPARE
quieter than · cleaner than · cheaper than · larger than · more peaceful than · more relaxing than · more modern than
SUPERLATIVE — -EST
the quietest · the cleanest · the cheapest · the largest · the busiest
SUPERLATIVE — THE MOST
the most peaceful · the most relaxing · the most modern · the most beautiful
SPECIAL
good → better → the best
GIVE A REASON
because it's ___. because you can ___. because there is / there are ___.
ASK MORE
Why do you recommend it? What can I do there? Why is it a good choice?

🟠 10 · Breakout Practice Instructions
Use this language 🗣️
VISITOR
I want a quiet place. I like history. I want somewhere modern. I want to relax. Which place do you recommend?
RECOMMEND
I recommend ___. You should visit ___. I think ___ is a great choice.
COMPARE
It's quieter than ___. It's cleaner than ___. It's cheaper than ___. It's more peaceful than ___. It's more modern than ___.
SUPERLATIVE
It's the quietest place. the cleanest · the most peaceful · the most relaxing · the most modern. It's the best choice for ___.
REASON
because you can ___. because it has ___. because there are ___.
VISITOR — ASK MORE
Why? What can I do there? Is it crowded?
REACT
That sounds good. Good idea. Thanks for the recommendation.
