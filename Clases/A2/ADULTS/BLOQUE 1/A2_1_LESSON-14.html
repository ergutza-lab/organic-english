📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Future Plan Toolkit 📚
ASK
What are you going to do? What are you going to do this weekend? Are you going to ___?
ANSWER
I'm going to ___. I'm going to ___ this week. I'm not going to ___.
PLAN IDEAS
study English, exercise, save money, visit family, start a course, learn a skill, rest, cook more, read more, practice English
TIME
tonight, this week, this weekend, next week, next month, this year
REASON
because I want to ___. because I need to ___. because it's important to me.
KEEP TALKING
Why? When? What else? Sounds good! Nice plan!
