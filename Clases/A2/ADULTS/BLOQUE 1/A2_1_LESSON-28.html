📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
A2.1 Communication Toolkit 📚
PAST
Last weekend, I... I went to... I visited... I was... First... Then... Finally...
FUTURE
I'm going to... I'm meeting... I'm working tomorrow. What are you going to do? What are you doing tomorrow?
ADVICE
You should... You shouldn't...
COMPARISON
___ is quieter than ___. ___ is more ___ than ___. I prefer ___ because ___.
HOTEL
I have a reservation. What time is breakfast?
AIRPORT
Which gate is my flight? What time does boarding start? Can you repeat that, please?
PROBLEM / HELP
I have a problem. My ___ doesn't work. I can't ___. Can you help me, please?
KEEP TALKING
Really? What about you? Why? Can you repeat that, please?
