THESE ARE TOOLS, NOT RULES. You don't need exactly seven turns.

📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Weekend Conversation Toolkit 📚
START
What are you doing this weekend?
SATURDAY
What are you doing on Saturday? I'm ___ on Saturday.
SUNDAY
What about Sunday? I'm going to ___ on Sunday.
ASK MORE
What time? Where? Who with? What else?
NO PLANS
I don't have plans yet. I'm free on Sunday.
REACT
Sounds good! Sounds busy! Sounds relaxing! Nice! Really?
RETURN
What about you? How about you?
