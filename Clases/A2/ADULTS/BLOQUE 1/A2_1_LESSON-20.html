👀 7 · Visual Focus
Doctor Visit Toolkit 📚
DOCTOR — OPEN
Hello. How can I help you? What's wrong?
PATIENT — START
I don't feel well. I feel sick.
PATIENT — SYMPTOMS
I have a headache. I have a cough. I have a sore throat. I have a stomachache. My throat hurts. I feel tired.
DOCTOR — ASK MORE
When did it start? Anything else? Do you have a cough? Does your throat hurt?
PATIENT — ANSWER
This morning. Yesterday. Yes, a little. No, I don't.
SIMPLE ADVICE
You should rest. You should drink some water. You should get some sleep.
CLOSE
Thank you. Take care. I hope you feel better soon.
LANGUAGE PRACTICE — NOT MEDICAL ADVICE

🧩 9 · Group Practice
Doctor & Patient 🏠
🩺
ROUND 1 · A = DOCTOR · B = PATIENT
Patient chooses ONE fictional card: A headache + tired · B sore throat + cough · C stomachache + dizzy · D back hurts + very tired
🅰️
DOCTOR
1. Greet. 2. Ask what's wrong. 3. Ask at least TWO questions. 4. Give ONE simple piece of advice. 5. Close.
🅱️
PATIENT
1. Explain the problem. 2. Give 1–2 symptoms. 3. Answer the questions. 4. Respond to the advice.
🔄 THEN CHANGE ROLES · Fictional information only.
