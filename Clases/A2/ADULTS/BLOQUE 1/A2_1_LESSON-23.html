🟠 13 · Free Production Instructions
Give a Clear Reason! 🗣️
CHOOSE
I prefer ___. I like ___ better. For me, ___ is better.
CONNECT
because...
DESCRIBE WHY
it's quieter · more peaceful · less crowded · more lively · cleaner · more modern · closer
ADD AN EXPERIENCE
I can relax there. I can walk there. There are more things to do. I feel comfortable there.
ASK
Why? What do you like about it? What about you?
REACT
Really? Me too. I see. That makes sense.
IF YOU GET STUCK
1. CHOOSE. 2. Say because. 3. Add ONE reason. 4. Ask your partner.
EXAMPLE
I prefer the countryside because it's quieter.
COMMUNICATION > MEMORIZATION
