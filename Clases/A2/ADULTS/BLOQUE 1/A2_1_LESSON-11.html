📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Useful Phrases 📚
TIME
In 2015... When I was 18... When I was a teenager... A few years ago... Last year...
EVENTS
I finished school. I started college/a course/my first job. I moved to ___. I started learning English. I visited ___. I learned ___.
ADD DETAIL
It was important because ___. I was nervous/excited. I learned a lot. It changed my life.
ASK
When did it happen? What happened? What is another important moment? Which happened earlier? Which is more recent? Why was it important?
KEEP TALKING
Really? That's interesting. What about you?

🟠 13 · Free Production Instructions
Now speak with classmates! 🗣️
MY IMPORTANT MOMENTS · Speak ~45-60s.
CHOOSE AT LEAST
3 important moments. For each: TIME + EVENT.
ORDER
Put them from earlier to more recent.
ADD
At least one extra detail about one moment.
EXAMPLE
In 2016, I finished school. In 2020, I started my first job. Last year, I started learning English.
Your partner listens and asks ONE follow-up question. Change roles. You do NOT need exact dates.
