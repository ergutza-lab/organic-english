📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Conversation Toolkit 🧰
START
Hi! How are things? How's everything?
LIFE / WORK / STUDY
What do you do? Do you work or study? Where do you work? What do you study?
FREE TIME / HABITS
What do you do in your free time? What do you do on weekends? How often do you ___?
REACT
Really? Nice! Interesting! Me too.
FOLLOW UP
Where? When? Why? What kind of ___? Do you like it? Can you tell me more?
RETURN
What about you? And you? How about you?
REMEMBER: Choose what helps the conversation.
