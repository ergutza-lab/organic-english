📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Useful Phrases 📚
ASK
What did you do yesterday? ...in the morning? ...in the afternoon? ...in the evening?
ANSWER
Yesterday, I worked. I watched ___. I cooked ___. I cleaned ___. I exercised. I studied ___. I visited ___. I listened to ___.
TIME
Yesterday morning... Yesterday afternoon... Yesterday evening... Last night...
KEEP TALKING
Really? Where? At home? What about you? And you?
REMEMBER: Simple actions are enough.
