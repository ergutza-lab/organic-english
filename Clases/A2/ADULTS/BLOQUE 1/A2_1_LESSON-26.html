📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Airport Information Toolkit 📚
START
Excuse me. Hi. Can I ask you a question?
GATE
Which gate is my flight? Where is Gate ___?
TIME
What time does boarding start? What time does the flight leave?
TERMINAL / PLACE
Which terminal is it? Where is the check-in desk? Where is baggage drop? Where is the information desk?
CONFIRM
Gate ___? Terminal ___? At ___? Did you say Gate ___?
ASK AGAIN
Sorry, can you repeat that, please? Can you say that again, please?
RESPOND
Okay. Great. Perfect. I see. Thank you.
STAFF ANSWERS
It's Gate ___. It's in Terminal ___. Boarding starts at ___. It's straight ahead. It's on the left / right. It's near ___.
