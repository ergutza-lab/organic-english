🟠 13 · Free Production Instructions
Check In and Ask 🗣️
Create ONE complete hotel check-in. About 60–90 seconds.
GUEST
say you have a reservation · give your name · ask at least THREE basic questions · respond politely
RECEPTIONIST
greet the guest · ask for the name · confirm the reservation · give a room number · answer the questions · close politely
THEN
CHANGE ROLES
Use fictional information.
