📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Useful Phrases 📚
ASK
How often do you ___? How often do you exercise?
ANSWER
I always / usually / often / sometimes / never ___.
ADD DETAIL
...in the morning. ...after work. ...on weekends.
KEEP TALKING
Really? Me too. What about you? When? Where? Why?
Remember: Frequency gives MORE INFORMATION.
