📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Plan-Making Toolkit 📚
SUGGEST
Let's have coffee. Let's have lunch. Let's watch a movie. Let's go for a walk.
TIME
How about Saturday? How about Sunday afternoon? What about 5:00?
ACCEPT
Sounds good! That sounds good. Great idea. Sure.
REJECT POLITELY
Sorry, I can't. Sorry, I'm busy. I'm working then. I already have plans.
CHANGE
How about Sunday? Maybe in the afternoon? What about 4:00?
CONFIRM
Perfect. Great. Sunday at 4:00. See you then.
