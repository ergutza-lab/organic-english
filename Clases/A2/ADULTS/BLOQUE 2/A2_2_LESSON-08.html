🟠 13 · Free Production Instructions
Now speak with classmates! 🗣️
ROUND 2 — TWO REAL EXPERIENCES EACH
1
Student A shares two real experiences, one at a time.
2
Student B asks when, where and how each experience was.
3
Student A answers. Student B reacts to each experience.
4
Switch roles. Student B shares two experiences; Student A asks.
Keep your answers short and natural. Use a new experience or reuse one from Round 1. Speaking time: 4 minutes
