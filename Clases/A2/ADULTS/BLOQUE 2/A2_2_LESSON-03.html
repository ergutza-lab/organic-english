📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Useful Phrases 📚
WHY — asks for a reason
“Why do you think that?”
BECAUSE — explains a reason
“Because I can review easily.”
FOR EXAMPLE — introduces an example
“For example, I practice every morning.”
BELIEVE — think something is true
“I believe daily practice helps.”
IN MY EXPERIENCE — based on what has happened to me
“In my experience, small steps help.”
USE THE PHRASES. DON'T MEMORIZE DEFINITIONS.
