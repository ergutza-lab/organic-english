📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Useful Phrases 📚
BEEN TO
visited a place at some time
“I've been to Puebla.”
ABROAD
in another country
“Have you traveled abroad?”
DESTINATION
a place you travel to
“My favorite destination is Oaxaca.”
TRIP
a journey to another place
“My favorite trip was to Oaxaca.”
FAVORITE
liked more than others
“It's my favorite city.”

✅ 15 · Feedback
LET'S IMPROVE! ✨
LANGUAGE UPGRADES
Use “to” with been
I have been Puebla.
I've been to Puebla.
Use the past for a specific time
I've been to Oaxaca last year.
I went to Oaxaca last year.
Say it naturally
I traveled to abroad.
I traveled abroad.
