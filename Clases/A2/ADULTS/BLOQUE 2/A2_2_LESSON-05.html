📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Useful Phrases 📚
HAVE YOU EVER
question used to ask about a life experience
“Have you ever been there?”
EVER
at any time in your life
“Have you ever traveled alone?”
NEVER
not at any time
“I have never tried that.”
TRIED
experienced or tested something
“I have tried a new sport.”
VISITED
gone to see a place
“I have visited several interesting places.”
ANSWER
“Yes, I have.” / “No, I haven't.”
