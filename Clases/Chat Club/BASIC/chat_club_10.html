6 · First Bag Conversation
FIRST BAG CONVERSATION
STUDENT A
Ask: What's in your bag?
Ask TWO more questions.
React.
Remember TWO objects.
STUDENT B
Answer in full sentences.
Say THREE objects.
Add ONE detail.
ROUND A — 3 min · ROUND B — 3 min · EXTRA TALK — 2 min. Then SWITCH.
Use: I have ___. · I also have ___. · Do you have ___? · Really? · Me too! · What else do you have?

→ I have keys.
In my bag have a notebook.
→ In my bag, I have a notebook.
Do you have charger?
→ Do you have a charger?
My partner have headphones.
→ My partner has headphones.
