4 · Listen and Remember
LISTEN AND REMEMBER!
Listen to a short profile. Remember FOUR things: NAME · AGE · PLACE · EXTRA DETAIL.
PROFILE A
"My name is Liam. I'm 31 years old. I'm from Ireland. I have one brother."
His name is Liam. He is 31 years old. He is from Ireland. He has one brother.
PROFILE B
"My name is Priya. I'm 27. I'm from India. I study."
Her name is Priya. She is 27. She is from India. She studies.
