5 · Favorite Place Conversation Toolkit
YOUR FAVORITE PLACE TOOLKIT
START
What’s your favorite place at home?
PLACE
My favorite place is ___.
DESCRIBE
It is: quiet · comfortable · nice · bright · small · big
WHAT’S THERE?
There is a ___. · There are ___.
WHAT DO YOU DO?
I read there. · I relax there.
I study there. · I watch TV there.
WHY? ⭐
I like it because it is ___.
I like it because I can ___ there.
ASK MORE
What is it like? · What is there?
What do you do there? · Why do you like it?
REACT
Really? · Nice! · Me too! · That sounds nice!
NEED HELP?
Can you repeat, please? · Slowly, please.
How do you say ___ in English?
CHOOSE: 1 PLACE + 2 DETAILS + 1 ACTIVITY + 1 REASON + 2 QUESTIONS
Can you repeat, please? · Slowly, please. · One more time, please. · What does ___ mean? · How do you say ___ in English?
