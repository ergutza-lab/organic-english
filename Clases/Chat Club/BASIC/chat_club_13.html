6 · First Food Conversation
TALK ABOUT FOOD
STUDENT A
Ask: What food do you like?
Ask about a favorite.
Ask about a drink.
React. Find ONE match.
STUDENT B
Answer in complete sentences.
Name at least TWO foods.
Name ONE drink.
Ask Student A too.
THEN SWITCH. USE: I like ___. · My favorite food is ___. · My favorite drink is ___. · Really? · Me too! · We both like ___.
ROUND A — 3 min · ROUND B — 3 min · EXTRA TALK — 2 min
BOTH STUDENTS SPEAK
What do you BOTH like?
Remember: FAVORITE FOOD · FAVORITE DRINK
