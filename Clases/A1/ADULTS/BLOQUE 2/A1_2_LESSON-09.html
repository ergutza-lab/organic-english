🟠 13 · Free Production Instructions
Find your hobby match! 🤝
Talk to a new partner. Find out:
1. What hobbies do they like?   2. Do they like reading?   3. Do they like cooking or exercising?   4. What activity don't they like?   5. What hobby do you both like?
✓ four questions✓ three hobbies✓ one negative preference✓ two reactions✓ one hobby match
Be ready to share one fact. Real or imaginary preferences — both are okay!
