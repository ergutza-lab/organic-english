🟠 13 · Free Production Instructions
Create your work or study profile! 📝
Talk to a new partner. Find out:
1. Do they work, study, or both?   2. What job do they choose?   3. What do they study?   4. Do they like their job or studies?   5. Is it busy or interesting?
✓ four questions✓ complete answers✓ one job or study detail✓ two reactions✓ How about you?
Be ready to share one fact. Real or imaginary information is okay!
