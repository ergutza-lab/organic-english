📚 8 · Clarification + Useful Vocabulary
Useful Phrases 📚
🔁
Again
Good to see you again.
= We met before. We meet another time.
🤝
Too
Good to see you, too.
= The feeling is the same for me.
📍
Now
I wake up at six now.
= This is my current situation.
➡️
Still
I still like tea.
= It was true before. It is true today.
Really?Nice!That's great!How about you?
