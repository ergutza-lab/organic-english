💬 5 · Communication Feedback
Communication Feedback 💬
Communication works when:
Clear ideas
Your listener understands your message.
Organization
Your ideas have a clear order.
Simple complete sentences
I work in an office. I like watching movies.
Active listening
You listen before asking or answering.
Communication strategies
Can you repeat, please? Can you speak slowly, please?
Confidence
You try, participate and keep going.
Continuing after mistakes
A small mistake does not stop your message.
Independence
Use less support when you are ready.
Communication works even when English is not perfect.
