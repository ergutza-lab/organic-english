Breakout Rooms! 🏠 · Describe a workplace.
🅰️
Student A
Ask:
1. Where do you work?
2. What is in your workplace?
3. Ask two more: Is it big? Is it busy? Are there computers? Do you like it?
🅱️
Student B
1. Answer with complete sentences.
2. Mention two or three objects.
3. Use two adjectives.
4. Ask Student A the same questions.
Change roles.
