📚 8 · Useful Phrases
Useful Phrases 📝
Say one object:

There is a lamp.   There is a bed.
Say more than one object:
There are two chairs.
There are three books.   There are two windows.
Ask:
Is there a table?   Are there chairs?
Answer:
Yes, there is.  •  No, there isn't.
Yes, there are.  •  No, there aren't.
